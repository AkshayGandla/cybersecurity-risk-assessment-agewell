# Cybersecurity Risk Assessment: AgeWell Innovations Digital Transformation

A written **security risk assessment** for a fictional aged-care provider (AgeWell Innovations) moving from paper to digital operations, with **AI-powered remote-access tools** and **partially managed personal devices** used by in-home carers.

**Full report:** [`report/AgeWell_Security_Risk_Assessment.pdf`](report/AgeWell_Security_Risk_Assessment.pdf)

## Approach
- **Context establishment**: business objectives (care quality, lower admin load, protection of sensitive health data, stakeholder trust) and legal constraints: Privacy Act 1988, Australian Privacy Principles, Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.
- **Qualitative, threat-based risk assessment**: each threat scored by likelihood x impact (Low / Medium / High) on a risk matrix.
- **Key risks identified**: unauthorised data access, AI misuse, data leakage, malware infection, phishing.
- **Treatments recommended**: Mobile Device Management (MDM), multi-factor authentication, monitoring of AI tool use, encryption, and staff cybersecurity training, chosen to be affordable and implementable inside the organisation's current structure.
- **Evaluation** section covering how residual risk and plan effectiveness would be reviewed.

## Skills demonstrated
Security risk assessment, threat modelling, risk matrices, governance and compliance (Australian privacy law), control selection (MDM, MFA, encryption), AI-use risk analysis, technical writing for board and technical audiences.

## Note
This is a written analysis of a case-study organisation, not a software project; there is no code in this repository.
